### q2 — Compute monthly gross sales by order date, refunds by refund date, and net sales for complete months only. (2 rows, 0 ms)

```sql
WITH ord_month AS (SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', order_date) AS month, SUM(amount_cents) AS gross_cents FROM orders WHERE order_date >= '2026-08-01' AND order_date < '2026-10-01' GROUP BY strftime('%Y-%m', order_date)), ref_month AS (SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', refund_date) AS month, SUM(amount_cents) AS refunds_cents FROM refunds WHERE refund_date >= '2026-08-01' AND refund_date < '2026-10-01' GROUP BY strftime('%Y-%m', refund_date)) SELECT months.month, COALESCE(ord_month.gross_cents, 0) AS gross_cents, COALESCE(ref_month.refunds_cents, 0) AS refunds_cents, COALESCE(ord_month.gross_cents, 0) - COALESCE(ref_month.refunds_cents, 0) AS net_cents FROM (SELECT '2026-08' AS month UNION ALL SELECT '2026-09') AS months LEFT JOIN ord_month ON months.month = ord_month.month LEFT JOIN ref_month ON months.month = ref_month.month ORDER BY months.month;
```
